# CS Hardware Day 1 - Measure

In this lesson, students are introduced to the fundamentals of electronics and circuit building. They learn how electricity works through the concepts of voltage, current, and resistance, and apply these ideas using Ohm’s Law. Students build a simple LED circuit first in Tinkercad and then on a physical breadboard, exploring the importance of resistors, polarity, and circuit safety. They also learn how to use a multimeter to measure voltage, current, resistance, and continuity, and practice troubleshooting common circuit problems. Finally, students extend their circuits using components such as potentiometers, buttons, and switches to control LED brightness.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

- Build a simple LED circuit in both Tinkercad and on a physical breadboard.
- Explain the relationship between voltage, current, and resistance using Ohm’s Law.
- Describe why resistors are used to protect components and control current.
- Identify the polarity of an LED and correctly orient it in a circuit.
- Distinguish between open circuits, short circuits, and functioning circuits.
- Safely use a multimeter to measure voltage, current, resistance, and continuity.
- Troubleshoot common circuit issues such as incorrect wiring, reversed polarity, and loose connections.
- Use resistors, potentiometers, buttons, and switches to modify circuit behavior and control LED brightness.
- Apply basic electrical safety practices when working with circuits and batteries.
